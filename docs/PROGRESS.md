## Next engineering slice

Prioritize small library calls that multiple titles can use. Good candidates are imports seen in more than one executable and operations with a short, independently testable contract. Compare each new operation against the reviewed DLL or a known output, and measure frequently called paths before optimizing them. The library bars track exported symbols. Texture formats, audio behavior and methods on graphics objects have their own tests. [Library coverage](LIBRARIES.md)

Frosty imports 60 symbols from 11 tracked Xbox DLLs. TuxBox covers 35 of them, including Frosty’s storage factory, all six D3D12 imports, the three CPU-side XMem physical-page calls and nine of the 15 GameChat delay imports. AcpHal builds seven imports that still need behavioral checks. Across the reviewed D3D12 DLL, 14 of 22 functions and all 32 exported identifiers have verified behavior. Graphics methods on the objects these functions return have separate tests. [Per-title coverage](LIBRARIES.md#frostys-imported-xbox-facing-apis)

On Linux, Frosty loads the Construct map and presents its main menu through TuxBox’s platform, graphics, input and media adapters. XG and the movie decoders come from the packaged Xbox system image. The graphics trace records 712-byte Xbox pipeline streams and a 3840 × 2160 display buffer. GPU tests cover indexed drawing, depth-plane writes that preserve stencil, and copies from native NV12 movie surfaces into Xbox textures. [Loaded libraries](../evidence/matrix-awakens/independent-menu.json) · [Graphics trace](../evidence/matrix-awakens/tuxbox-menu-startup.json) · [GPU checks](../runtime/d3d12/README.md#what-runs-on-the-gpu)

The particle-sort fix passed in the retail title. City vista, lighting and chase gameplay layers activate. Interactive gameplay remains unverified.
